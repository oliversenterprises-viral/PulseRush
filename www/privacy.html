PulseRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

PulseRush is a reflex arcade game. This policy explains what the app stores and what third parties may process if you install the Android version.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your display name, high scores, coin balance, owned skins, streak, and sound/haptic preferences in local storage on your device. We do not create an account and we do not operate a player database.

Challenge links

If you share a score, the link contains only the score, a short name you chose, a date, and the game mode. Anyone with the link can see that challenge.

Advertising (Android)

The Google Play build may show Google AdMob ads (including rewarded video and interstitial ads). Google may collect advertising identifiers, coarse location, and device/usage data as described in Google’s privacy policy. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings. The web version of PulseRush does not load AdMob.

Permissions

PulseRush does not require contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. Vibration is used for optional haptics. The share sheet is used only when you tap Challenge a friend.

Children

PulseRush is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed at children under 13. Do not create challenge names that include personal information about a child.
