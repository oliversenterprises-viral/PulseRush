# Closed testing roster

Google requires personal Play developer accounts created after 13 Nov 2023 to run a **closed test with 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days** before production.

Recruit **14–16** people. Twelve is the floor; opt-outs reset the clock.

## How testers must join

1. You create the app in Play Console and upload `store/PulseRush-1.0.0.aab`.
2. Start a **closed testing** track.
3. Add testers by Gmail address.
4. Send them the Play Console **opt-in link** (not a random APK and not the Vercel URL).
5. They must stay opted in for 14 days. Testers who leave early do not count.

The web game at https://pulserush-six.vercel.app/ is for growth. It does **not** count toward the 12-tester rule.
