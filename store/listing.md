## Full description

PulseRush is a one-thumb neon arcade built for “one more try.”

A pulse races toward the target ring. Tap when they overlap. Perfect hits ignite your combo. Miss three times and the run is over — unless you continue.

Then the real game starts: challenge a friend.

After every run you can share a score card and a link. They open it, see your number, and have to beat it. If they win, they share it back. That’s the loop.

Features
• Endless mode that gets faster the better you play
• Daily seed — the same pattern worldwide each UTC day
• Fever mode when your combo catches fire
• Unlock neon skins with coins from your runs
• Challenge links you can drop in chat, SMS, or socials
• Optional ads on Android (rewarded continue + occasional interstitial)

No account. No clutter. Just the pulse.
